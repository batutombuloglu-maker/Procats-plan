# Knowledge Treasury 1.7.4

## Scheduled review

The Review tab is an in-app inbox of due cards. Correct answers advance through intervals of **1, 3, 7 and 30 calendar days**, followed by a 30-day interval for every later correct answer. The intervals are measured from the latest scheduled review, not from the original creation date. A Hard or Forgot answer always schedules the next calendar day and resets progression; the next successful review starts at the 1-day step.

Cards become due at the beginning of their scheduled day in the local timezone when the date is calculated. Answered cards leave the due list. Upcoming cards display their next date. The badge refreshes when returning to the app and while the knowledge view is open. This is an **in-app reminder inbox**, not a newly added Android/iOS system-notification service.

Existing content and backup fields are preserved. Older review metadata is interpreted under the new interval sequence without rewriting the archive on launch. Changing the question, answer or image still resets a card to immediately due. Keep all active devices updated to avoid older clients using their previous scheduling behavior.

## Independent discovery

Random discovery has its own search, type, category and tag filters. Its selections do not modify the archive filters. Mixed discovery remains available. Answers in discovery are session-only: **none of the three grades changes a due date, review counter or cloud record**. A forgotten card may reappear once within that discovery round.

## Content types and overlapping subjects

Grouped choices cover words, quotes, poems, books, films, TV series, games, artworks, music, scientists, artists, people, history, events, geography, places, mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, science, philosophy, literature, technology, psychology, mythology, economics and languages.

One card can belong to several subjects. For example, choose Scientist for Newton and select Physics and Mathematics under Related subjects. The same card appears in each matching filter, including random discovery. The main type uses the existing card format/category path; additional subjects use the existing tags. Category paths and free-form tags remain available. Existing limits remain 5 category levels and 12 combined subjects/tags per card. Excess subject selections are reported instead of silently discarded.

The archive still renders at most 36 cards per page, searches all records, and retains its 2,000-card / 2,000,000-byte limit. This is a personal archive with manual entries; no preset content or AI service was added.

## Personal distribution and recovery

Android personal package: `com.procats.study.personal`, version 1.7.4, phone code 24, watch code 25. Phone and watch must share the application ID and original signing certificate. Existing personal installations can be updated in place. Keys and passwords are not in this repository. Windows builds provide installer and portable EXEs. Firebase Hosting updates the web client; bundled APK/EXE interfaces require a new installed package.

Before updating, export an encrypted Data Vault backup. Keep independent copies and the password separately. Source-code history does not contain or back up an individual's tasks, notes or cards. Custom background photos are device-only and need separate originals. The offline recovery tool can decrypt a backup to standard JSON without Firebase. Recovered JSON is unencrypted private data and must not be committed.

No Play Store upload or live Firebase deployment is performed by preparing these files.
