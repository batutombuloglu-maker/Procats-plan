# Geography atlas sources

Country outlines and city coordinates: Natural Earth 1:10m and 1:50m, public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). Country labels/metadata: mledoze/countries, ODbL 1.0 (https://github.com/mledoze/countries, https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). This derived country metadata collection is available under ODbL 1.0. Flag rasters: Flagpedia/FlagCDN and Wikimedia Commons (Afghanistan white flag), see the card-pack sources.

Population and surface area: World Bank WDI SP.POP.TOTL and AG.SRF.TOTL.K2; latest available 2020–2025 observation as downloaded 1 November 2026. Taiwan population: Ministry of the Interior, December 2025 household register. Vatican residents: Vatican City State, 31 December 2024. Population definition and reference year vary by source; the year is displayed. Each fact retains its source URL.

Map geometry is bundled application content. Cards store only a stable atlas reference, so backups do not need large bitmap duplicates. Maps are cartographic illustrations, not assertions about disputed borders or recognition. Outlying dependencies are omitted from some detail views; tiny archipelago detail focuses on the capital island. This atlas version must remain available when later app versions are released.
